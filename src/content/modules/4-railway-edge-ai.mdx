# EC8707: High-Speed Railway Edge AI & Optical Track Defect Classification

<div class="ieee-abstract">
  <strong>Abstract</strong>—Automated railway track condition monitoring demands millisecond defect classification to prevent catastrophic derailments on high-speed rail corridors. This module presents an edge-deployed artificial intelligence platform combining an NVIDIA Jetson Nano edge accelerator and ESP32-CAM satellite nodes. Utilizing an 8-bit integer quantized MobileNet-V2 convolutional backbone, the system classifies surface fissures, missing elastic rail clips, and ballast irregularities at 45 frames per second. We evaluate high-speed rolling shutter shear distortions and validate vibration anomaly fusion.

## I. TinyML Model Quantization & TensorRT Edge Pipeline

Deploying convolutional models on embedded platforms requires compressing FP32 weights into 8-bit integers (INT8) to utilize SIMD vector units:

$$q = \text{round}\left( \frac{r}{S} \right) + Z$$

Where:
- $r$ is the real FP32 value
- $S$ is the positive floating-point scale factor: $S = \frac{r_{\max} - r_{\min}}{q_{\max} - q_{\min}}$
- $Z$ is the integer zero-point offset

```text
[ OV2640 Image Sensor ]
         |
         v DMA Capture (RGB565 / Grayscale)
[ Frame Buffer Queue (FreeRTOS) ]
         |
         v 224x224 Bilinear Resize
[ TensorRT INT8 Engine ] ---> [ Class: Cracked Rail (98.4%) ]
         |
         v Trigger Telemetry
[ LTE Cat-M1 / SQLite Logger ]
```

## II. The Engineering Trap: Rolling Shutter Geometric Shear & Track Vibration Glitch

<div class="glass-card p-5 rounded-2xl border border-red-500/40 bg-[#041026]/90 space-y-3 my-6">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-2 text-red-400 font-mono font-bold text-sm tracking-wide">
    <span class="text-base">⚠️</span>
    <span>ENGINEERING TRAP: THE ROLLING SHUTTER TRACK SHEAR & JITTER SKEW TRAP</span>
  </div>

  <p class="text-xs text-slate-200 leading-relaxed font-sans">
    When deploying standard CMOS camera modules (such as the low-cost OV2640 on ESP32-CAM) beneath a train inspection bogie moving at $120 \text{ km/h}$, students discover that the neural network's detection accuracy plummets from <strong>$96.8\%$ on the lab bench down to $12.3\%$ on live track trials</strong>.
  </p>

  <div class="p-3 rounded-xl bg-[#020813] border border-red-500/30 text-xs font-mono text-slate-300 space-y-2">
    <div class="text-amber-400 font-bold">1. Rolling Shutter Row Readout Lag:</div>
    <p>
      In a rolling shutter CMOS sensor, pixel rows are exposed and read out sequentially from top to bottom. For an image height of 1080 lines with a line readout time of $28.5 \text{ µs}$, the time delta between the top row and bottom row is $\Delta t = 30.8 \text{ ms}$. At $v = 120 \text{ km/h}$ ($33.3 \text{ m/s}$):
    </p>
    <div class="text-center py-1 text-red-300">
      $$\Delta x_{\text{displacement}} = 33.3 \text{ m/s} \times 0.0308 \text{ s} \approx 1.03 \text{ meters!}$$
    </div>
    <p>
      Straight, parallel steel rails are captured as curved trapezoidal hyperbolas, distorting spatial feature convolutions and yielding false negatives.
    </p>

    <div class="text-amber-400 font-bold pt-1">2. High-Frequency Bogie Jitter:</div>
    <p>
      Track rail joint impacts generate vertical accelerations exceeding $15 \text{ g}$ at $120 \text{ Hz}$, producing high-frequency horizontal banding across the exposed frames.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="p-3 rounded-xl bg-[#00693E]/20 border border-[#00F0B5]/30 text-xs font-mono text-slate-300 space-y-1">
    <div class="text-[#00F0B5] font-bold">✓ Anna University Laboratory Mitigation Protocol:</div>
    <ul class="list-disc list-inside space-y-1 text-slate-300">
      <li><strong>Global Shutter Camera Mandatory:</strong> Transition from rolling shutter sensors to global shutter sensors where all pixels expose simultaneously.</li>
      <li><strong>Pulsed LED Strobe Illumination:</strong> Deploy high-intensity 850 nm infrared LED arrays pulsed for only $15 \text{ µs}$ per frame, freezing motion blur to less than $0.5 \text{ mm}$.</li>
      <li><strong>Inertial Sensor Gating (ADXL345):</strong> Buffer optical frames and cross-reference with acceleration peaks. If track vibration spikes, flag the image for multi-frame optical flow alignment.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
